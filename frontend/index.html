Welcome, Volunteer!

Thank you for contributing to our Arabic Sign Language dataset. Your recordings will help train machine learning models to recognize sign language.

How It Works

  1. Enter your name
  2. Watch a reference sign video
  3. Record yourself performing the same sign
  4. Your video is automatically uploaded
This will be used to name your video files

Recording Guidelines

Good Conditions

  • Plain background (white or light grey)
  • Good lighting from front or side
  • Camera on stable surface or tripod
  • Dark clothing (black, navy, dark grey)
  • Upper body visible (waist to head)
  • Both hands visible at all times

Avoid These

  • Busy or cluttered background
  • Light behind you (backlit)
  • Handheld/shaky camera
  • Bright or patterned clothing
  • Hands leaving the frame
  • Other people in the video

Timing Structure (5 seconds total)

Pause 1-2s
Sign 1-3s
Pause 1-2s

Important Warning

If the sign you perform does not match the reference video, the dataset becomes incorrect and can damage the machine learning model. Please verify you are performing the correct sign before recording.
